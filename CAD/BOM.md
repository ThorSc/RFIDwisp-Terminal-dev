What do you need to build the RFID wisp terminal?

1. 1 x WT32-SC01PLUS (Wireless-Tag Technology Co., Limited)
1. 1 x RC522 RFID Module (MFRC-522)
1. 1 x KCD11 2 Pin 10x15mm Mini Push Button Switch
1. 1 x USB Jack 3.1 Type-C 4 P Welding Wire 3A Female DIY Connector Straight
1. 1 x USB 3.1 Type C Male Plug
1. 1 x 3mm LED blue
1. 1 x R680 Ohm resistor
1. Wires AWG24 for connection between WT32 and RC522
1. Wires AWG18 or 0.75mm² for power lines
